Setup, once per machine

  1. Extend your desktop across both monitors (not "duplicate").
  2. Open Output 1 and Output 2 above, each in its own browser window.
  3. Drag each window onto its monitor, then press F11 (or the on-screen fullscreen button) to go fullscreen.
  4. Keep the Console open on your own laptop or a third window — changes push to both outputs instantly.
